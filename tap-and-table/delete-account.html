Tap & Table — Delete your account

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Tap & Table and sign in.
  2. Go to More → Account.
  3. Tap Delete my account and enter your MPIN to confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request by email

If you can't use the app, email atharvaprimes@gmail.com with the subject "Tap & Table account deletion" and include:

  • the mobile number you sign in with,
  • the business name, and
  • whether you want to delete only your account or the whole business (whole-business requests must come from the business admin).

We confirm by phone or email that the request is yours and complete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: your name, mobile number and MPIN are permanently removed and the account can no longer sign in.
  • Whole business: the business profile, all staff accounts, items, stock, purchases, vendors, orders and bills.

What is kept

When only a single account is deleted, the bills and stock records it created stay with the business, because businesses must keep them under tax and accounting laws. They are no longer linked to your name or mobile number.
