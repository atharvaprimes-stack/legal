Tap & Table — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Tap & Table ("the app", "we", "us") is a point-of-sale and inventory app for restaurants, bars and wine shops. It is used by business owners and their staff. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and how you can have it deleted.

1. Who this app is for

The app is a business tool. Accounts are created by a business owner (admin) or by us when the business is set up. It is not intended for consumers or for anyone under 18.

2. Data we collect

DataWhy
Your name and mobile number To identify you and let you sign in. Your mobile number is your login ID.
Your 4-digit MPIN To sign in. It is stored only as a one-way bcrypt hash, never in readable form.
Business details (name, licence number, address) Shown on bills and used to keep each business's data separate.
Sales, bills, orders, stock, purchases, and which staff member recorded them The core purpose of the app: running and reporting on the business.
Supplier (vendor) names and optional phone numbers entered by the business To record stock purchases.
A push notification token for the device To alert staff when a guest places an order from a table QR code.

Guests who order through a table QR code are not asked for any personal information; only the table number and the items ordered are recorded.

3. Device permissions

  • Microphone — only when you tap voice search. Speech is turned into search text by your phone's speech recognition service (for example Google). We do not record or store audio.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth) and Location — only to find and connect to a Bluetooth receipt printer. Android requires location permission for Bluetooth scanning on many devices. We do not read, store or send your location.
  • Notifications — to show new table order alerts.

4. Where data is stored

Data is stored on your device (so the app works offline) and synced to our cloud database hosted on Supabase. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each business can only access its own data.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Data is shared only with the service providers needed to run the app (Supabase for database hosting, Expo/Google for push notifications), or when required by law.

6. Retention

Account data is kept while the account is active. Bills, sales, stock and purchase records belong to the business and are kept for as long as the business uses the app and as required by applicable tax and accounting laws.

7. Deleting your account and data

  • In the app: open More → Account → Delete my account and confirm with your MPIN. Your name, mobile number and MPIN are permanently removed and the account can no longer sign in.
  • Without the app: follow the steps on our account deletion page.
  • Closing a whole business: the business admin can request deletion of the business and all of its data through the account deletion page.

When a staff or admin account is deleted, the bills and stock records it created remain with the business for legal record-keeping, but are no longer linked to the person's name or mobile number.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children and is meant for licensed businesses and their adult staff.

9. Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date at the top.
