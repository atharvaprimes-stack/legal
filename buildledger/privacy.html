BuildLedger — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

BuildLedger ("the app", "we", "us") is a sales, stock and customer-dues ledger for building-material shops, made by ATHARVA PRIME SOLUTIONS. It is used by shop owners and their counter staff. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and how you can have it deleted.

1. Who this app is for

The app is a business tool. Shop accounts are set up by us, and the shop owner adds their staff. It is not intended for consumers or for anyone under 18.

2. Data we collect

DataWhy
Your name and mobile number To identify you and let you sign in. Your mobile number is your login ID.
Your 4-digit PIN To sign in. It is stored only as a one-way hash by our authentication provider, never in readable form.
Shop details (name, address, GSTIN, licence number, optional GPS location and logo) Shown on invoices and statements, and used to keep each shop's data separate.
Customer details entered by the shop (name, mobile number, village or city) To record sales, advance wallet balances and outstanding dues for each customer.
Sales, orders, payments, stock, restocks, and which staff member recorded them The core purpose of the app: running and reporting on the shop.
Vehicle and driver details for the shop's own fleet To record which vehicle delivered an order.
Crash and error reports To find and fix bugs. Reports contain technical details about the error and device. Phone numbers, email addresses and GSTINs are removed from reports before they leave the device.

Shops are responsible for having a lawful reason to record their customers' details in the app. We process that information only to provide the app to the shop.

3. Device permissions

  • Location — only when the shop owner chooses to save the shop's GPS location in the shop profile. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Photos — only when the shop owner picks a shop logo. Only the selected image is uploaded.
  • Microphone — only when you tap voice search to find a customer. Speech is turned into search text by your phone's speech recognition service (for example Google). We do not record or store audio.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth) and USB — only to find and connect to a receipt printer.
  • Fingerprint / Face ID — optional, to unlock the app after it locks. Biometric checks happen entirely on your device; we never receive your biometric data.

4. Where data is stored

Data is stored on your device (so the app works offline) and synced to our cloud database hosted on Supabase. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and your sign-in session is kept in the device's secure storage. Each shop can only access its own data. Local shop data is cleared from the device when you sign out.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use advertising SDKs. Data is shared only with the service providers needed to run the app (Supabase for database and file hosting, Sentry for crash reports, Expo for app updates), or when required by law.

Invoices and statements are created on your device. When you share one on WhatsApp or another app, it is sent by you through that app; we do not send messages on your behalf.

6. Retention

Account data is kept while the account is active. Sales, customer, payment and stock records belong to the shop and are kept for as long as the shop uses the app and as required by applicable tax and accounting laws.

7. Deleting your account and data

  • Staff accounts: the shop owner can deactivate a counter seller at any time from Settings → Staff, which immediately stops that account from signing in.
  • By email: email atharvaprimes@gmail.com with the subject "BuildLedger account deletion", and include the mobile number you sign in with, the shop name, and whether you want to delete only your account or the whole shop (whole-shop requests must come from the shop owner). We confirm the request is yours by phone or email and complete it within 30 days.

Deleting an account permanently removes your name, mobile number and PIN, and the account can no longer sign in. Deleting a whole shop removes the shop profile, all staff accounts, customers, products, stock, orders, payments and vehicles. When only a single account is deleted, the sales and stock records it created stay with the shop for legal record-keeping, but are no longer linked to your name or mobile number.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children and is meant for businesses and their adult staff.
